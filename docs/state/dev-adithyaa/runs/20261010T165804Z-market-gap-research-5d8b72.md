## Work performed

Searched more than two dozen queries across consumer visa tools, mixed-passport
planning, document readiness, application scheduling, transit, airline disruption,
meeting location optimization, event operations and travel grants. Opened Travel
Buddy's supplied page and relevant primary provider documentation. This was a
public-web discovery review, not exhaustive market coverage or authenticated
product testing. Marketing descriptions are provider claims, not verified service
quality. Search absence cannot prove nonexistence.

Found substantially closer overlaps than the preceding recommendation captured:
canwego already advertises mixed passports, held documents, budgets, cost estimates,
preparation tiers and per-person official links. Jettova advertises fairness-based
meeting location optimization, availability and passport filters. Visafar includes
next-best-document suggestions, provenance and transit. VisaCheck AI advertises
document consistency checks; VisaMind and VisaMet advertise sequenced timelines.
VisaHQ already handles event attendee visa workflows. VisaCRM describes passport
custody and multi-application sequencing. Voyasee offers transit risk and route
comparison; Nexa offers airline re-accommodation. Therefore none of those broad
categories should be presented as novel.

Candidate narrower workflows with no close full public product found in this
search: event inclusion intervention planning under a support budget; consumer
multi-application passport custody scheduling with delay scenarios and conflict
explanations; pre-booking document-aware disruption rehearsal with minimum-change
repairs. Each has adjacent competitors and substantial validation remaining.
Event inclusion is a different target audience and requires user agreement before
changing product scope. Model feasibility and uncertainty, never approval odds or
guaranteed attendance. Funding cannot remove a legal restriction or ensure a visa.
